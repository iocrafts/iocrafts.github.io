Welcome to IOcrafts — your gateway to innovative, community-driven software solutions. 
At IOcrafts, we believe in the power of open source: making our code freely available so anyone can use, modify, and contribute to our projects. 
This approach fosters collaboration, transparency, and rapid innovation, benefiting both developers and organizations of all sizes.

Current projects:

- [**Hsftp**](projects/hsftp/) — a command-line SFTP client written in Haskell for scripted uploads and downloads, with filtering by date and file extension.
- [**Puppetchef**](projects/puppetchef/) — a Node.js tool that runs browser automation recipes written in YAML on top of Puppeteer, with support for custom plugins.
